About ChronoEarth

ChronoEarth is an educational web project developed by a team of four B.Tech CSE students at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. Built entirely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it demonstrates the application of modern frontend development principles to scientific storytelling.
