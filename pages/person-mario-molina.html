Mario Molina

1943 – 2020 · Chemist

With F. Sherwood Rowland he showed in 1974 that CFC gases can destroy the ozone layer. They shared the 1995 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

What they contributed

  • Co-authored the 1974 paper in Nature, “Stratospheric Sink for Chlorofluoromethanes: Chlorine-Atom-Catalysed Destruction of Ozone”, with F. Sherwood Rowland.
  • Showed that chlorine released from CFCs high in the atmosphere destroys ozone, and that one chlorine atom can destroy about 100,000 ozone molecules.
  • Shared the 1995 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Rowland and Paul Crutzen.
  • His research led to the Montreal Protocol, the first international treaty to tackle a global environmental problem of human origin.
