Rachel Carson

1907 – 1964 · Marine biologist and writer

Her book Silent Spring (1962) alerted the public to the dangers of careless chemical pesticide use and became one of the most influential books of the modern environmental movement.

What they contributed

  • Wrote a sea trilogy: Under the Sea-Wind (1941), The Sea Around Us (1951, which won a National Book Award) and The Edge of the Sea (1955).
  • Published Silent Spring in 1962. It challenged the use of chemical pesticides such as DDT and warned of their effect on birds and other wildlife.
  • President John F. Kennedy’s Science Advisory Committee accepted her scientific findings, and her writing helped lead to stronger pesticide controls.
  • Her work is credited as partly responsible for the creation of the US Environmental Protection Agency in 1970. The EPA cancelled DDT in 1972.
  • Was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom after her death, in 1980.
