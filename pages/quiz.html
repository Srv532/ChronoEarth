Question 1 of 8 · Formation of the Earth

The oldest known terrestrial minerals, dated to about 4.4 billion years, come from the Jack Hills of Western Australia. What are they?

💡 Show hint

These tiny crystals have oxygen isotope ratios that suggest liquid water existed on Earth surprisingly early. They are also used in uranium-lead dating.

✅ Correct! Zircon crystals from the Jack Hills are the oldest known terrestrial minerals (4.4 billion years). Chondrite meteorites, by contrast, date the solar system at 4.567 billion years. 📖 Learn more

❌ Not quite. The answer is Zircon crystals. Zircon crystals from the Jack Hills are the oldest known terrestrial minerals (4.4 billion years). Chondrite meteorites, by contrast, date the solar system at 4.567 billion years. 📖 Learn more
