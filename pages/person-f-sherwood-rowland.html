F. Sherwood Rowland

1927 – 2012 · Chemist

A chemistry professor at the University of California, Irvine, he discovered with Mario Molina that CFCs destroy the ozone layer, and won the 1995 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

What they contributed

  • With his postdoctoral student Mario Molina, found that a single chlorine atom from CFC products such as aerosol sprays could destroy 100,000 ozone atoms in the stratosphere.
  • Endured years of criticism of the finding before it was accepted.
  • Shared the 1995 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Molina and Paul Crutzen.
  • Was a founding professor of chemistry at UC Irvine, working on atmospheric chemistry and chemical kinetics.
